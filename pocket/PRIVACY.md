# Rhema Pocket — Privacy Policy

Rhema Pocket does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Your writing is stored as plain files on your device. If you have iCloud
Drive turned on, those files also live in your own iCloud account, under
iCloud Drive › Rhema Pocket, so that Rhema on your Mac can open the same
books. Apple carries those files between your devices; Rhema Pocket never
sees them leave your device and has no server of its own.

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no
third-party services. It makes no network requests.

Rhema Pocket is part of the Rhema family project (based on Hugh
Howey's NEO, MIT), which goes public at ship time — until then, questions
go to the family.

*Last updated October 2026.*
